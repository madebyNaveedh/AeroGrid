# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2021/664 on the Regulatory Framework for the U-space

*European Union Aviation Safety Agency (EASA) Reference Standards*

**Regulatory Domain:** Global U-space & UTM Best Practices

---

## Article (3) - Mandatory U-space Services

U-space airspace mandates four fundamental services: Network Identification Service, Geo-awareness Service, UAS Flight Authorization Service, and Traffic Information Service. Real-time deconfliction must occur within seconds.

## Article (7) - Priority Rules for Emergency Aircraft

UAS operators and U-space service providers must immediately accommodate priority flights, including manned emergency medical services (HEMS), search and rescue, and disaster relief flights.
