# UAE Federal Decree-Law No. (45) of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (PDPL)

*UAE Data Office - Federal Personal Data Protection Framework*

**Regulatory Domain:** Data Privacy & Algorithmic Governance

---

## Article (5) - General Principles of Personal Data Processing

Personal data collected by autonomous systems and drone payloads must satisfy the principles of lawfulness, purpose limitation, and data minimization. Video feeds collected during urban flight must not be stored in identifiable format unless strictly necessary for authorized emergency or legal purposes.

## Article (13) - Automated Redaction and Pseudonymization

Data processing pipelines must implement privacy-by-design: automated blurring, facial anonymization, and removal of personal identifiers at the collection node (PP node) before transmission or persistence.

## Article (18) - Retention Periods and Accident Investigation Balance

Data retention classes must align with incident severity. Routine operational logs must be deleted within established retention schedules, while incident-related telemetry must be preserved securely for review.
