# ITU AI Ready - Analysis Towards a Standardized Readiness Framework (Report 2.0, Jan 2026)

*ITU AI for Good Global Summit & Standards Study Group*

**Regulatory Domain:** AI Governance & National Readiness Dimensions

---

## Dimension 1 - Data and Model Marketplace

Requirements for standardized schemas, model validation benchmarks, and guardrails against generative hallucination.

## Dimension 7 - Strategy Alignment & Institutional Intent-Splitting

Multi-agent frameworks must translate high-level national intents (e.g. 'keep emergency corridors safe and connected') into granular sub-intents allocated across distinct institutional mandates (aviation authority, telecom operator, fleet).

## Dimension 10 - AI and Policies (Explainability & Auditability)

Mandates transparent chain-of-thought logging, source attribution to national legislation, and cryptographic audit trails so every autonomous recommendation is legally explainable and verifiable.

## Dimension 13 - Digital Infrastructure & Energy Characteristics

AI pipeline nodes deployed at edge collectors and cell sites must record computational execution times and energy draw.
